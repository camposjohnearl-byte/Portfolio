About me

I'm John Earl Campos, a web developer and designer based in Lisbon. I care about interfaces that are quick, clear, and kind to everyone who uses them.

Over seven years I've worked with startups, agencies, and nonprofits. I like owning a project end to end: shaping the idea, designing the screens, and writing the code that ships.
